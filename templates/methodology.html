In-season rankings

Overall is the primary power rating and the center of in-season per-simulation FPI draws. With no FBS games played it equals the preseason combined rating. As teams play more FBS games, Overall gradually shifts toward the algorithm margin (No Preseason) on the schedule shown in the rankings table. Rank movement (▲/▼) compares each team’s Overall rank to the prior week.

No Preseason shows the pure algorithm margin (capped at ±40) from completed FBS-vs-FBS results, with no preseason component.

Resume starts from each team’s Overall margin and adjusts it so that expected win percentage on completed FBS-vs-FBS games (using the same logistic win model as in-season simulations) matches a Bayesian-smoothed record: (wins + 1) / (games played + 2). Each team is calibrated independently: opponents keep their Overall rating during the adjustment. The rating moves by 0.01 points per iteration until expected and Bayesian win rates are within 0.01 or 1,000 iterations are reached. Teams with no FBS games played use Overall as Resume.

Forecast Resume uses the same calibration loop as Resume, but the target win rate is forecasted wins ÷ forecasted games played (mean wins and mean games from the current in-season simulations, including a conference championship game when the team reaches it). Opponents still use Overall. Teams with no completed FBS games use Overall. Projected wins are from the latest sim run, so historical weeks in the dropdown use that snapshot’s forecast with each week’s completed games through that week.

In-season projections

Each run models {{ meta.sim_count }} possible finishes to the {{ meta.season_year }} season from the current week forward. Completed games are pinned to their actual results in every simulation. Remaining games are simulated using per-simulation FPI draws centered on Overall with uncertainty that shrinks as teams play more FBS games: σeff = 7.3 when no FBS games have been played; otherwise 7.3 / √(FBS games played). Each simulation’s draw (not the Overall column itself) is used for remaining game margins, playoff seeding, and bracket matchup strength.

Key metrics

Overall margin
Primary rating blended from preseason and in-season results by games played. Centers each season’s simulated FPI draw; bracket odds use the draw, not this point estimate.
Resume margin
Overall adjusted to align sim-style expected win% on completed FBS games with Bayesian-smoothed win% (see In-season rankings above).
Forecast Resume margin
Same as Resume, but the target win rate is forecasted season wins divided by forecasted games played from in-season simulations.
No Preseason
Algorithm-only margin rating from completed FBS-vs-FBS results.
Record
Overall W–L from completed games on the master schedule (all opponents).
Forecasted wins
Average regular-season wins across all {{ meta.sim_count }} in-season simulations.
SOS
Strength of schedule: average opponent rating across scheduled games with home/away/neutral site adjustments.
Playoff / Title odds
Chance of making the 12-team playoff or winning the national title from the current state of the season.

Playoff selection & bracket

Traditional sims (1–{{ meta.traditional_sim_count or meta.sim_count }}): four power autobids, one Group of 5 autobid (playoff points), seven at-large spots by wins/SOS/H2H rules; seeds follow that ranking (G6 is seed 12).

Resume playoff sims ({% if meta.resume_playoff_sim_count %}{{ meta.traditional_sim_count + 1 }}–{{ meta.sim_count }}{% else %}when enabled{% endif %}): same game outcomes as the paired traditional sim, but selection uses end-of-season resume margins — P4 champs, highest-resume G6, Notre Dame if top 12 by resume, then highest resume for remaining slots; seeds by resume (G6 still #12).

Within each simulation, bracket matchup win probabilities use that simulation’s FPI draw, not the Overall column on the rankings table.
