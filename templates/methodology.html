In-season rankings

Overall is the primary power rating and drives in-season simulations. With no FBS games played it equals the preseason combined rating. As teams play more FBS games, Overall gradually shifts toward the algorithm margin (No Preseason) on the schedule shown in the rankings table. Rank movement (▲/▼) compares each team’s Overall rank to the prior week.

No Preseason shows the pure algorithm margin (capped at ±40) from completed FBS-vs-FBS results, with no preseason component.

In-season projections

Each run models {{ meta.sim_count }} possible finishes to the {{ meta.season_year }} season from the current week forward. Completed games are pinned to their actual results in every simulation. Remaining games are simulated using per-team Overall rating draws with uncertainty that shrinks as teams play more FBS games: σeff = 10 / (1 + √(FBS games played)).

Key metrics

Overall margin
Primary rating blended from preseason and in-season results by games played. Used for in-season sim FPI draws.
No Preseason
Algorithm-only margin rating from completed FBS-vs-FBS results.
Record
Overall W–L from completed games on the master schedule (all opponents).
Forecasted wins
Average regular-season wins across all {{ meta.sim_count }} in-season simulations.
SOS
Strength of schedule: average opponent rating across scheduled games with home/away/neutral site adjustments.
Playoff / Title odds
Chance of making the 12-team playoff or winning the national title from the current state of the season.

Playoff selection & bracket

Same 12-team rules as our preseason model: four power autobids, one Group of 5 autobid, remaining at-large spots by eligibility rules, then the official bracket format with byes for seeds 1–4.
